Decisions

IDChoice · Rationale
D-001Set 15.0. macOS Sequoia identifies as version 15.
D-002Replace macOS 26 glass APIs. They block compilation against the Sequoia SDK; regular content and material preserve the interaction.
D-003Use @preconcurrency for Contacts. Its SDK concurrency annotation conflicts with the existing main-actor store and no app concurrency changes are required.
D-004Replace both .buttonStyle(.glass) calls with .bordered. The unavailable style otherwise manifests as a four-minute Swift type-check timeout.
D-005Use @preconcurrency for QuickLookThumbnailing. Its non-Sendable thumbnail result prevents the Sequoia SDK build.

  • A-001 · The removed glass treatments are cosmetic; the existing controls and interactions remain unchanged.

Reversibility

R-001 · low. Restore 26.0 in the generator and plist, then regenerate the project.
